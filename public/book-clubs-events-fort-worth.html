The room, by the hour

Your book club, on the rug — $50 an hour.

Bluebonnet Books & Wine is Fort Worth's third place with a reading habit: clubs already meet here, Dana hosts them, and readers are encouraged to start their own and claim the space.

Hosted & bring-your-own

Two ways to get a club into the room.

Dana hosts. The shop runs the club for you — already happening, with the photos on Instagram to prove it. Hosted clubs pay $50 an hour and bring in whatever they want to read, eat or share.

You host. Already have a club? Bring it. Dana built the shop as a home for clubs that need a home, and she wants more — book clubs for kids are planned, and bookstore-sponsored clubs are coming in the new year.

On the calendar

What actually happens in this room.

December 2026

Poetry class with typewriters

A Fort Worth ISD teacher who just published a poetry book hosts a two-hour writing class at the shop — she teaches her process, then everyone writes. Paper and typewriters provided: type your poem and take it home as a gift or a memento.

Thursdays

Friday Friends

A former Montessori teacher spends Thursday mornings at the shop with the little ones — and usually ends up holding babies, to the delight of everyone in the room.

Community night

ACH foster-family event

ACH Child and Family Services used the shop for an open evening where anyone could walk in and ask what fostering actually looks like. That's the kind of night this room is for.

In the works

Kids' camps & new clubs

Crafts, journaling, playtime and snacks are planned for kids' camps, more children's book clubs are on the way, and bookstore-sponsored clubs arrive in the new year.

Dates and sign-ups get posted on the shop's Instagram and Facebook page.

Why a bookstore hosts at all

"I wanted this to just be a fun, easy space."

Dana talks about the shop as a third space — the place that's neither home nor work, where a mom can put a kid down and say "we needed to get out today," and the answer is perfect, this is the space for it.

"You need your community, so I'm hoping that's what the store comes to."

Dana St. Germain, as told to the Fort Worth Report · November 2026
