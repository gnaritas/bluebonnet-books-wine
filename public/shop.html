What's in the shop

Books first. Antiques second. A glass, soon.

One 800-square-foot room near TCU, furnished like a living room: shelves of cheap good books, used furniture and antiques between them, a table of little gifts — and book clubs renting the whole vibe by the hour.

Aisles one through everything

Used books — $5, $10, $20

Novels and memoirs, biographies, cookbooks, children's titles, plus off-the-beaten-path finds Dana hand-picks because somebody forgot them.

Between the shelves

Furniture & antiques

The shop is laden with used furniture, antiques and embellishments — the Fort Worth Report called the room "laden" and meant it. If a piece fits your room, take it home.

By the register

Small gifts from $1

Fun stickers, shirts, and little notebooks with colored pencils inside for a dollar — priced so a kid with pocket change can leave happy too.

In planning

Wine & beer

The "wine" in the name is a plan, not a promise yet: kegged pours and bottles by the glass are coming once the shop's beer-and-wine license is approved, per CultureMap Fort Worth.

How the shop works

No algorithms, no surge pricing, no pressure.

Every book lives in one of three price tiers — $5, $10, $20 — so shopping is calm for kids and cheap for everyone. If you ask for a book the shop doesn't have, expect Dana to hand you "that book adjacent" instead. That's not a sales pitch; it's how she told the Fort Worth Report she curates.
