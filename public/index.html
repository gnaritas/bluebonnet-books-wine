The whole shop, priced in three tiers

Every book has a price you'd say yes to.

Owner Dana St. Germain grew up with a middle-school librarian mom — and opened the shop so no kid ever hears "that book costs too much." If everything costs a few dollars, picking is easy and everyone goes home happy.

$5

Paperbacks, chapter books and kids' picks — yes, sometimes nicer chapter books too.

$10

Middle-shelf finds, from memoirs to cookbooks, priced so a browse never hurts.

$20

The special picks — the top of the price ladder is still twenty dollars.

"I didn't ever want to tell my kids, 'I don't want to buy you a book,' just because of the price. If they want to read, I want to feed that."

Dana St. Germain, as told to the Fort Worth Report · November 2026

On the shelves and in the bins: novels and memoirs, biographies, cookbooks, children's picture books and chapter books — plus whatever fun turned up in the first order (a Tiger King memoir made it).

A third place

Book clubs pay $50 an hour — and bring their own snacks.

Dana calls the shop a third space: not home, not work, just a good room with good books. Clubs already meet here; readers are welcome to start their own.

  • Hosted book clubs — $50 an hour, groups bring whatever they want to read, eat or share
  • December 2026: a two-hour poetry class with a Fort Worth ISD teacher and author — paper and typewriters provided, you type your poem and take it home
  • Friday Friends — a former Montessori teacher helps with the little ones Thursday mornings
  • Community nights, like the ACH Child and Family Services event on what it means to foster

This book made me think of you

Gift a book without knowing which one.

Tell Dana about the person — what they're going through, what they loved last — and she picks the book, wraps it, and the note in your words goes inside. Monthly pick-up at the shop. It started with a husband wanting to surprise his wife; it became the shop's favorite program.

Run by a Fort Worth family

Family-owned, kids at the register.

Dana St. Germain — an Iowa native raised by a school librarian — left a corporate sales career to open the shop with her husband Robert Costas, a TCU alum. Their kids, 11 and 8, both know how to run the point of sale; Dana's niece Elizabeth, fresh from a Washington bookstore, stops by most days.

The shop is within walking distance of local schools, and TCU students wander in constantly. Kids hang out after school, parents grab books, and somebody always ends up reading in the corner. That's the point.

Good questions

Asked at the counter, answered here.

Where exactly are you?

3464 Blue Bonnet Cir #120, Fort Worth, TX 76109 — in the loop of Blue Bonnet Circle near TCU, in an 800-square-foot shop that used to be an optometrist's office.

When are you open?

Wednesday through Saturday, 10am to 6pm — plus private bookings whenever you need the room.

What do books cost?

Every book is $5, $10 or $20. Small gifts start at $1. Hosted book clubs pay $50 an hour and bring whatever they want.

Do you sell wine yet?

The name says so — pours are planned once the shop's beer-and-wine license is approved, per CultureMap Fort Worth. Books, antiques and gifts are in stock right now.

Can my book club meet here?

Yes — hosted clubs run $50/hr. Bring your own snacks and your own reading; Dana also encourages readers to start new clubs in the space.
