Plain-words privacy

What this site knows about you is very little.

Bluebonnet Books & Wine is a used bookstore, not a data machine. Here's the whole policy, in the same spirit as the $5 price tier.

What we collect

The only thing this site collects is what you type into the contact form: your name, your email, an optional phone number, and your message. That's it. There are no sign-up popups and no cookie banner, because there are no cookies of ours to banner about.

What it's used for

Answering you, and only answering you: a book request, a club booking, a gifting question. Your message goes to the shop's email. We don't add you to mailing lists you didn't ask for, and we never sell what you send.

No trackers

This site carries no analytics scripts, no advertising tags, and no third-party JavaScript at all. The one exception is the embedded Google map on the visit and contact pages: when those maps load, Google may set its own cookies under its own privacy policy, not ours.

Who to ask

Questions, or want what you sent deleted? Email dana@bluebonnetbookshop.com, call (817) 948-8436, or ask at the counter — 3464 Blue Bonnet Cir #120, Fort Worth, TX 76109, Wednesday through Saturday, 10am–6pm.
